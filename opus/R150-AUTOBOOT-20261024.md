## Hardware

1. SD (`scripts/r150-sd-install.sh`, from 4.19): new folder `tb371fc-linux/autoboot-6.12`
   with Image, board.dtb, initramfs.cpio.gz, SHA256SUMS, ENABLE; all three re-read and
   hash-verified after drop_caches. Nothing else on the card was touched.
2. Flash via `temporary-vbmeta-recovery-test.ps1 -Action LaunchFastboot`. The first run was
   wrapped in `*>&1`; PowerShell 5.1 turned fastboot stderr into a terminating error right
   after "Sending recovery_b OKAY" and left fastboot's USB endpoint hung (getvar timeouts).
   The user replugged USB and restarted from the bootloader menu; the tablet booted recovery.
   **Never redirect stderr of this controller.**
3. The booted recovery was R150 (the write had completed): console 4.19 → countdown →
   autoboot kexec → 6.12 R149b, boot 6304ad1d-d856-4b63-8724-cc8bd9d241d0, 8 CPUs,
   r130-usb/r130-sshd/r149-touch/r149-plasma active, touch calibrated, KDE on tty7.

## How to use

- Power on / reboot: desktop comes up by itself (~3.5 min incl. 4.19 stage).
- To stay in the 4.19 console: press any button or touch the screen during the countdown.
- To disable autoboot permanently: delete `tb371fc-linux/autoboot-6.12/ENABLE` on the card.
- A new payload needs both a new SD folder content and an R15x recovery with new pinned hashes.

## Not verified

- A full cold power-off → power-on cycle has not been tested yet (only reboot into recovery).
- recovery_b contents were proven by behaviour, not by readback.
