## Why panel is not enabled

Live R131 DT disables both dsi@ae94000 and dsi@ae96000, their panel and PHY
paths. Panel compatible currently points to BOE + generic novatek fallback,
but clean R118's nt36523 driver has no TB371FC match, and backlight class is
disabled in kernel. Enabling DT alone cannot solve that.

Archived v24 stock DT includes `nt36532 tianma 3k video mode dsi panel`.
Existing verifiers passed on current repository:
- verify-mainline-tianma-command-table.py: 88 commands, 86 data payloads,
  800 stock bytes, waits and reset sequence match.
- verify-mainline-display-timing.py: 2944x1840@120 dual DSI, derived clock
  1,127,664,000 vs stock1,130,000,000 (2067ppm).
- verify-mainline-dsc-pps.py: all128 captured PPS bytes match.

These validate archived data and patch text, NOT the physical panel variant
in the current running first-stage DT or successful initialization on6.12.
The existing mainline panel patch needs --recount and does not apply unchanged
to the clean6.12 driver (context mismatch at original line279).
Do not apply a guessed BOE init table or blindly unreserve GPIOs.
